A LETTER FROM MY HEART

Omofolahan Mi,

I have been trying to find the perfect words to tell you just how much I love you, but honestly, I don't think words will ever be enough to completely explain what you mean to me.

I love you deeply. I love you in ways that go beyond the sweet messages, the laughter, the beautiful moments, and everything we share. I love the person you are, your heart, your smile, your voice, your little habits, your personality, and even those little things you probably don't realize I notice.

You have become such a beautiful and important part of my life. Your presence brings a kind of happiness into my heart that is difficult to explain. Sometimes, just seeing your name on my phone is enough to make me smile.

Sometimes I find myself thinking about you randomly, and I realize how lucky I feel to have someone as special as you in my life.

Omofolahan, I want you to know that my love for you isn't based only on the good days. I don't only want you when everything is perfect. I want to be there through the difficult days too.

I want to understand you, listen to you, support you, annoy you a little sometimes 😂, make you laugh, and remind you that you are loved.

Thank you for being in my life. Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every memory, every little moment, and every time you've made my heart feel full.

I don't take you or what we have for granted.

I love you, Omofolahan. More than these words can explain, more than one letter can contain, and more than I probably know how to express sometimes.

And if I have to remind you every single day, I will:

I LOVE YOU.
I CHOOSE YOU.
I CHERISH YOU.
AND I AM GRATEFUL FOR YOU. ❤️

Forever your boy,
Bukunmi ❤️
